# Review of documents in sources

Reviewed: 21 November 2026. Eight PDFs, 359 PDF pages. Text was extracted across all files; contents and selected relevant sections were read in detail. Representative soil-workflow, dashboard and crop-stage pages were also rendered and visually inspected. This is a product/workflow review, not an agronomic validation of every table, product or claim. Page references below are PDF viewer page numbers; some files contain two printed pages per PDF page.

## Proposed additions to the existing backlog

These are planned, not implemented by this review. Reuse AGR-01–08 in [Agrii review](AGRII_REVIEW.md), existing field reports, tasks and visits; avoid parallel competing record systems.

| ID | Farmer wording and useful result | Implementation and acceptance requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRC-01 | What problem am I trying to fix? | Connect an objective (for example, standing water), dated checks/photos/test papers, agreed jobs and a return visit. Preserve earlier findings; show unknown outcome and who supplied advice. Extend existing records rather than copy them. |
| SRC-02 | My soil checks over time | Field/point, sampling date, depth, lab/method, value and unit. Show comparable measurements as separate dated series; distinguish organic matter from organic carbon and do not merge incompatible tests. Missing tests remain empty. Build on current report attachments. |
| SRC-03 | What stage is my crop at? | Original or properly licensed crop-specific diagrams with plain labels and optional technical code behind help. Stage is farmer-confirmed, not inferred from a photo or sowing date. Local crop review required; unknown remains available. |
| SRC-04 | My trees and repeat harvests | Planting/block identity, optional known planting date, tree counts with source/date, recurring pruning/maintenance, separate harvest rounds and losses. Current coconut/garden records only provide a foundation. Support concurrent batches explicitly before claiming mixed-crop support. |
| SRC-05 | Water and equipment | Optional tanks, pumps and drains linked to areas; capacity, manual level/volume reading with date/unit/source, inspection and maintenance tasks. Shared tank readings must not be counted once per linked field. Sensors are a separate integration. |
| SRC-06 | Seed I bought and planted | Seed lot/variety/supplier, packet/test germination date and source, quantity with units, planting use and stock movements. Tie to seed calculator and inventory workstream. Entered rates remain estimates until actual use is recorded. |
| SRC-07 | What worked on this field? | Side-by-side recorded work, inputs, measured harvest and income/cost completeness by comparable planting. Flag different years/areas/methods. Observational differences do not establish treatment effectiveness. External benchmarks need permissions and comparable local data. |

## Dashboard and navigation implications

- Keep Home for purpose/setup/next action; Dashboard for the whole farm; field detail for one growing area's evidence and work.
- First show attention-needed jobs, last check and dated photo, then crop-specific detail. Current dashboard already supplies the base; new source ideas are not yet data-backed widgets.
- Add soil trends only when compatible dated tests exist. Add harvest and earnings graphs only after structured actual harvest/sales records exist. Keep coconut counts, grain kg and flower bunches separate.
- Later water/weather panels must display source and observation/forecast time; no connected sensor labels for manually entered data.
- A Resources area should filter by crop, task, region and review date. Present short illustrated explanations first and the original reference second. Keep downloaded reference material distinct from farmer evidence.

## Applicability and reuse limits

These documents largely describe UK systems, commercial services and products, and include older material. Do not turn their cultivar rankings, UK approvals, application rates, disease models or soil classification thresholds into Salem advice without current local evidence and expert review. No source images, proprietary tables or commercial text were copied into the app. Local PDF availability is not permission to redistribute them in a public web bundle; retain references separately and verify rights before publishing assets. No source claim establishes prediction accuracy for FieldSense.
